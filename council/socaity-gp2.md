# Council: socaity-gp2 + socaity-vwy (consolidated) — Entity staging and grant signature: who signs, what to incorporate, when

Participants: legal-counsel, grant-writer
Type: decision · Priority: P1 (calendar-critical: blocks socaity-4we, the first NLnet application) · Consolidates socaity-vwy (identical decision, identical participants)

Issue (merged):
- Context: grants are the M0–M2 bridge; contracts need a clean counterparty; one founder, NL-based. NLnet can contract individuals; STF and foundations generally prefer/require entities. The stichting/BV foundation-split is explicitly deferred (sustainability.md names trigger conditions) — but the signature question cannot wait.
- Question: (a) Does the founder sign the first NLnet MoU as a natural person, or does an entity come first? (b) If/when an entity: BV or stichting — which form keeps grant inflows compatible with the ledger commitment (inflows distributed per recorded contribution), limited liability, VAT identity, and the M5 escrowed-marketplace future? (c) The timing trigger: incorporate before which event?
- Why it matters: entity choice under deadline pressure is how projects acquire structures they regret; retrofitting a counterparty after grant award is painful; incorporation is cheap and reversible-ish.

Adopted context binding this council:
- socaity-19p resolution noted: a BV with founder-as-DGA triggers gebruikelijk-loon (deemed salary independent of the ledger) — "one more argument for the stichting-first path"; entries must stay contingent/non-transferable/defeasible (no present-day tax event).
- socaity-1ux: no special founder position; uniformity covenant; the eventual foundation/service split stays a named monitor item with trigger conditions — this council decides the BRIDGE entity, not the M5 structure.
- socaity-x8o/zjr: grants are ledgered and distributed-or-reserved under the rule from day one; distributions discretionary until declared.
- Legal knowledge layer: "BV preferred once grants land (limited liability, clean grant counterparty, VAT identity)"; grant compatibility: NLnet fine with individuals or BVs; STF contracts with the maintaining entity.

## Round 1

### [legal-counsel]
PROPOSAL (owner). *(Analysis, not legal advice; two items need a licensed practitioner.)* **Resolving my own knowledge-layer tension: I withdraw "BV preferred once grants land" — it was a generic default written before two adopted constraints existed.** A single-founder BV makes the founder a DGA with aanmerkelijk belang → gebruikelijk loon (≈€56k/yr deemed salary, Wet LB art. 12a) levied regardless of actual cash flow — a mandatory present-day tax event decoupled from the ledger rule (violates 19p's no-present-tax-event posture) and a structurally special founder position (salary channel nobody else has; violates 1ux's uniformity in spirit). **Stichting-first.**
**(a) NLnet MoU: founder signs as natural person, now.** NLnet contracts individuals routinely, pays against milestones; incorporation must not gate the application. Proceeds ledgered under the x8o/zjr rule from euro one; novate to the entity later (routine for NLnet). Never sign anything STF-shaped as a natural person.
**(b) Bridge entity: stichting, with the future BV as an M5 subsidiary.** Decisive: no shares → no aanmerkelijk belang → no deemed-salary regime; founder can work unpaid or be paid only via the uniform ledger rule. Grant counterparty: stichting is the PREFERRED form for STF/NGI/foundations (public-benefit optics, no profit motive). VAT: registers when performing economic activity; pure grants typically outside scope. Liability: full legal personality, board shielded absent mismanagement (art. 2:9 BW) — equivalent to BV for the bridge; the escrowed-marketplace future runs through a PSP and/or the M5 BV subsidiary anyway. ANBI: available to stichting, categorically unavailable to BV — don't apply now, keep statutes ANBI-compatible. Governance at N=1: one-person bestuur is lawful; statutory commitment to expand to ≥3 before ANBI application or first above-de-minimis distribution. **Conversion trap runs the other way:** a stichting cannot convert to a BV, but the M5 target (Blender pattern) puts a stichting ON TOP — stichting-first means M5 restructuring = incorporate one subsidiary; BV-first means reparenting/dissolving at split time, the expensive path. Forward-compatible, not a detour.
**The hard wrinkle (blocking before first founder distribution): uitkeringsverbod (art. 2:285(3) BW)** — a stichting may not distribute to founders/board. Standard mitigation (Blender pays developers): ledger-rule payments structured and papered as compensation for work performed, never profit distribution — the retroactive-grants framing already points this way. Statute wording + ledger-rule/compensation interface to a licensed NL notary/counsel before any founder distribution, not before incorporation.
**(c) Trigger:** NLnet award notification OR the start of any entity-requiring application, whichever first; hard backstop "before the second grant contract of any kind." ~€500–800 + KvK; do not incorporate before award (an empty stichting is dead weight and a filing obligation).
**PARAMOUNT:** no entity form creating a deemed-income tax event on contingent ledger entries (sole-founder bridge BV refused); no entity-requiring contract signed by a natural person; no stichting→founder distribution before counsel clears the uitkeringsverbod/compensation structure and statutes.

### [grant-writer]
**(a) NLnet + natural persons: confirmed** — individuals are routine; MoU = milestone task list; **re-papering mid-grant is cheap with NLnet specifically** (grantee→entity novation handled before; an email exchange and amended MoU, not a renegotiation; continuity of the responsible person is what they care about). **(b) STF/STA: entity strongly preferred but not our near-term counterparty** — STA is the observatory's *customer* from M2, not an M0–M1 funder; their entity requirement sets the deadline at "before the first STA conversation turns contractual" (M2-ish). **(c) Later funders:** foundations effectively require a nonprofit-ish entity or fiscal sponsor — stichting satisfies directly; a BV forces fiscal-sponsor workarounds; the no-token rationale reads far better issued by a public-benefit foundation. Everything after NLnet gets easier with a stichting on the letterhead. **(d) Sequencing confirmed: apply now, incorporate in parallel, zero conflict** — the NLnet form does not require an incorporated counterparty at submission; legal identity settles at MoU stage, 3–6 months post-submission; a stichting takes ~a week and €400–700. **The sequencing risk runs entirely the other way: waiting for incorporation to submit risks missing the call for nothing.** (e) **Entity form IS a funder-facing asset, and it's the stichting:** no shareholders + statutory public-benefit purpose makes the commons commitment and no-token stance *structural claims* rather than promises — program officers read that as mission lock-in; a BV reads as "startup keeping options open," inviting exactly the extraction question we'd argue away.
**PARAMOUNT:** no sequencing that delays the NLnet application pending incorporation; no BV as the grant-receiving bridge entity presented to public-benefit funders.
CONSENT to any resolution incorporating the application-first sequencing and these refusals.

### Facilitator
Full convergence in Round 1 — both roles independently reached: apply/sign NLnet as natural person now; stichting as the bridge entity (BV refused by both, for tax-structure and funder-optics reasons respectively); BV only as the M5 service subsidiary; incorporate at award/entity-requiring-trigger; novation as the fallback. Trigger phrasings are compatible (award notification precedes MoU signature). Round 2 = pro-forma cross-confirmation.
