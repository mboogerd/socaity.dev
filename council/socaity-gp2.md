# Council: socaity-gp2 + socaity-vwy (consolidated) — Entity staging and grant signature: who signs, what to incorporate, when

Participants: legal-counsel, grant-writer
Type: decision · Priority: P1 (calendar-critical: blocks socaity-4we, the first NLnet application) · Consolidates socaity-vwy (identical decision, identical participants)

Issue (merged):
- Context: grants are the M0–M2 bridge; contracts need a clean counterparty; one founder, NL-based. NLnet can contract individuals; STF and foundations generally prefer/require entities. The stichting/BV foundation-split is explicitly deferred (sustainability.md names trigger conditions) — but the signature question cannot wait.
- Question: (a) Does the founder sign the first NLnet MoU as a natural person, or does an entity come first? (b) If/when an entity: BV or stichting — which form keeps grant inflows compatible with the ledger commitment (inflows distributed per recorded contribution), limited liability, VAT identity, and the M5 escrowed-marketplace future? (c) The timing trigger: incorporate before which event?
- Why it matters: entity choice under deadline pressure is how projects acquire structures they regret; retrofitting a counterparty after grant award is painful; incorporation is cheap and reversible-ish.

Adopted context binding this council:
- socaity-19p resolution noted: a BV with founder-as-DGA triggers gebruikelijk-loon (deemed salary independent of the ledger) — "one more argument for the stichting-first path"; entries must stay contingent/non-transferable/defeasible (no present-day tax event).
- socaity-1ux: no special founder position; uniformity covenant; the eventual foundation/service split stays a named monitor item with trigger conditions — this council decides the BRIDGE entity, not the M5 structure.
- socaity-x8o/zjr: grants are ledgered and distributed-or-reserved under the rule from day one; distributions discretionary until declared.
- Legal knowledge layer: "BV preferred once grants land (limited liability, clean grant counterparty, VAT identity)"; grant compatibility: NLnet fine with individuals or BVs; STF contracts with the maintaining entity.
