# Council: socaity-x8o — Publish the epoch-share distribution rule as code before the first external ledger entry

Participants: mechanism-designer, legal-counsel, platform-engineer
Type: decision · Priority: P0 · Blocked by (open): socaity-19p (pricing heterogeneous contributions, founder rate), socaity-9cb (model the earliness premium curve) — this council may fix the architecture and delegate parameter values to those issues.

Issue:
- Context: vision.md "Credit" and milestones.md Principle 3 commit to an append-only ledger from day one with retroactive distribution in proportion to recorded contribution, with an epoch-share earliness premium; M0 is done when the ledger records its first external contribution. But the rule itself is unspecified: epoch boundaries and length, the pie schedule per epoch, how epoch-share and absolute pricing coexist, and the exact denominator of "recorded contribution".
- Question: What is the exact, machine-executable distribution rule — epoch length, pie schedule, epoch-share vs absolute interaction, rounding, and the retroactive formula — and can it be published as code alongside the first ledger entry?
- Why it matters: Retroactive-distribution ambiguity is the single most reliable source of community blowups. Every ledger entry accepted before the rule is fixed is a claim of unknown value.

Adopted context binding this council (do not relitigate):
- socaity-zjr (Ledger Validation Policy): provisional→confirmed lifecycle; confirmation fixes eligibility + relative weight, never amounts; pre-distribution audit blocking; challenged entries escrowed at declared weight; capped audit slice (placeholder 5%).
- socaity-1ux (Standing Commitment): rule ships at M0 as versioned executable code, authoritative over prose; formula STRUCTURE final for every epoch opened under it; parameter values amendable only for not-yet-opened epochs via a meta-rule itself published at M0; declaration discretionary; allocation binding; non-transferable; founder on the same rule at a declared rate.
- socaity-7mk: entries attributed to permanent keypair identities; observations vs valuations separated (socaity-zyt discipline): observations immutable, valuations recomputable by replay.
- Deterministic replay (platform): fixed-point/rational arithmetic, no wall-clock, byte-equal recomputation by strangers (forkability CI).

## Round 1

### [mechanism-designer]
PROPOSAL (owner).
**1. Epochs:** consecutive, gapless, fixed-length in ledger time, indexed e=0,1,…; clock anchors to the genesis entry (M0 publication of this rule); contributions assigned to epochs by the timestamp of their immutable observation, never confirmation time (late confirmation changes eligibility, never placement). Pre-genesis observations → epoch 0, with the full founder position itemized in the M0 publication so any contributor sees the epoch-0 denominator before joining. Epoch length L is a parameter (default recommendation to socaity-9cb: 91 days).
**2. Pie schedule — no explicit premium multiplier:** each epoch has pie P_e in claim units (cu), a pure function of epoch index, published as code, non-increasing in e (structure). Recommended value: constant P_e = P — constant-pie-with-growing-denominator IS the earliness premium, magnitude set by actual network growth rather than founder forecast. If 9cb shows insufficiency, the only admissible shape is a decaying schedule (one knob); a separate multiplier column is refused.
**3. One denominator:** every observation declares quantity in native units (human hours, agent tokens, compute units); published conversion schedule V (versioned code) maps to valuation units: 1 vu = 1 standard contributor-hour; founder's declared rate is just V applied to founder hours, same table as everyone. V fixed per epoch before it opens (structure); rates in V delegated to socaity-19p. D_e = Σ w over confirmed contributions assigned to e; challenged entries at declared weight in escrow; D_e final only when epoch closed AND zjr audit run.
**4. Two modes:** at declaration each entry carries an irrevocable mode election, A (absolute: compute credit w×r, closed-loop, NO distribution claim) or E (epoch-share: exact rational w/D_e, claim of P_e×w/D_e cu). Default during bootstrap: E. **ALL confirmed contributions enter D_e regardless of mode** (else electing A pumps E-holders' shares — a coordination game against the commons); forgone fractions lapse, never roll forward (rollover creates late-epoch pie-pumping). Retroactive distribution: snapshot closed+audited epochs; R_i = Σ_e P_e×(w_{i,e,E}/D_e); inflow net of capped audit slice distributed ∝ R_i. Open epochs never participate.
**5. Arithmetic:** weights as integer micro-vu; shares as exact rationals; no floating point anywhere. Quantized payout: floor to quantum, allocate residual by largest remainder, ties by ascending entry id; payouts sum exactly to the distributed total on every replay.
**6. Structure vs parameter:** structure = §1 mechanics, §3–4 formula, mode-neutral denominator, irrevocable election, lapse rule, non-increasing pure-function pie, §5 arithmetic. Parameters = L, P_e values (→9cb), V rates incl. founder rate + tokens-per-vu, absolute rate r (→19p), audit-slice cap.
**PARAMOUNT:** no explicit earliness multiplier stacked on shrinking-share; no post-confirmation mode switching; no mode-dependent denominator; no floats/wall-clock/iteration-order dependence; no denominator term from self-declared capacity fractions; no retroactive edits to P_e/V_e/D_e composition for any opened epoch — errors in opened epochs are borne, itemized, corrected only in unopened ones.

### [legal-counsel]
Incremental constraints beyond 1ux: **(a) Pie schedule publishes fine IF fractions, never amounts:** allocation function (declared X → fractions) fully concrete; declaration event outside the rule. A schedule in absolute forward-looking amounts ("epoch 3 = €N" or "N redeemable credits") converts it into a quantified promise — where participation-right characterization gets traction. As fractions, concreteness *strengthens* the position (pre-committed non-manipulable grant formula). **(b) Absolute mode is the closer of the two modes to the cliff edge:** wage-like rate is fine per se (wet DBA turns on instruction-authority, not rate stability), but the code must not encode delivery obligations, exclusivity, or platform-directed assignment as accrual conditions; and **non-transferability must be enforced in the rule itself — no transfer operation exists in the code** (keypair-bound balances per 7mk), not merely stated in prose. If transferability is ever added, absolute-mode credit crosses into e-money/MiCA first. **(c) Rounding is an unfair-terms surface:** remainder must go to contributors or a published neutral sink, never silently to platform/founder; it is formula structure, unamendable for opened epochs — get it right once. **(d) Founder rate is the largest self-dealing surface in scope:** (i) published parameter, amendable only prospectively; (ii) rate declaration cites a verifiable external referent (market benchmark or grant-funder day-rate) in covering documentation — cheap evidence against a self-dealing narrative; (iii) **socaity-19p must resolve before the first epoch opens** — an unresolved placeholder rate opening an epoch is a self-dealing claim of unknown size.
**PARAMOUNT:** no schedule stating future distributions as amounts rather than fractions of discretionary declarations; no transfer operation in the rule-as-code for either mode; no opening the first epoch with the founder rate unresolved or retroactively amendable.
CONSENT to proceed on these terms. (Not legal advice; wording review and characterization opinion remain with licensed counsel per register.)

### [platform-engineer]
Constraints (1ux code-authoritative and zyt observations/valuations taken as given): **(1) Language:** rule module = pure function (ledger_export, rule_params) → payout_table; zero third-party dependencies, stdlib only; recommendation **Python 3 stdlib with fractions.Fraction** (pinned "any CPython ≥3.10"); no I/O, wall-clock, env, randomness, or dict-order dependence (sort by entry hash before iterating); one reference implementation declared authoritative, golden vectors make any future second implementation verifiable. **(2) Arithmetic:** exact rationals; floats banned and AST-lint-enforced; rounding exactly once at final materialization; remainder rule fully specified so the table sums exactly to the declared amount — largest remainder, deterministic tie-break **by entry hash**. **(3) Replay contract, published at M0:** given ledger export at a named checkpoint hash + declared amount/cutoff + rule version per epoch → byte-identical payout table on any machine; canonical JSON (RFC 8785-style: sorted keys, no floats) for input and output; payout-table hash is itself a ledger event; validation-state snapshot (challenged/escrowed entries at declared weight) is part of the input, not a side channel. **(4) Version binding in-band:** rule_version = hash(source + canonical params); events RuleVersionPublished, EpochOpened(epoch_id, rule_version_hash), EpochClosed(epoch_id, checkpoint_hash), MetaRulePublished; amendments are RuleVersionPublished events that the meta-rule's own validity predicate must accept (target epoch unopened, structure unchanged, procedure followed). **(5) Harness ships with the rule at M0:** ≥10 golden-vector fixture ledgers (incl. single contributor, zero-weight epoch, all-challenged epoch, remainder ties, founder-rate entry); property tests (conservation exact, permutation invariance, no-float AST, determinism); forkability CI from public artifacts only in a clean container, green before the first distribution and running on golden vectors before real data. **(6) M0 scope:** before FIRST ENTRY — event schema + version field, observation/valuation separation, keypair attribution, hash chain, rule + meta-rule published with green CI, EpochOpened for epoch 1. Before FIRST DISTRIBUTION (M5-ish) — audit-blocking workflow, declared-amount plumbing, checkpoint mirrors, optional second implementation. Rule module plausibly <500 lines. **PARAMOUNT:** no floats in the mechanism path; no dependency outside pinned stdlib in the replay path; **no first external ledger entry before the rule-version hash and EpochOpened are in the log**; no balances-as-source-of-truth anywhere; no prose-over-code or aspirational byte-identity.

### Facilitator
Standing proposal: MD's rule architecture §1–6 + PE's engineering envelope (Python stdlib reference implementation, replay contract, in-band version binding, harness, M0 scope) + legal's constraints (fractions-not-amounts, no transfer op, remainder-to-contributors, founder-rate preconditions).
Deltas for Round 2: (i) **cu semantics vs legal's fractions-not-amounts paramount** — MD's P_e is denominated in "claim units"; distribution is ∝ R_i (relative), so cu appear to be weights, not amounts — MD to confirm explicitly that cu are never redeemable at a fixed value and the published schedule reads as relative weights; legal to confirm that satisfies the refusal. (ii) **remainder tie-break** — MD: ascending entry id; PE: entry hash. Pick one. (iii) **rounding mode** — PE offered half-even or truncate (MD's call); MD's §5 floor+largest-remainder — confirm floor as the named rule. (iv) **19p-before-epoch-1** as an explicit precondition in the resolution (legal paramount) — MD/PE confirm sequencing works with PE's "no first entry before EpochOpened" (entry #1 requires epoch 1 open, which requires V rates incl. founder rate resolved). (v) Mode-A accrual conditions: MD to confirm the code encodes no delivery obligation/exclusivity/assignment conditions (legal b).
Open objections: none standing; (i) is the only potential paramount collision and looks terminological.
