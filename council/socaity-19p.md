# Council: socaity-19p — Pricing heterogeneous M0–M2 contributions with no market: valuation rule and the founder rate-setting conflict

Participants: mechanism-designer, community-builder, legal-counsel
Type: decision · Priority: P1 (elevated in practice: HARD M0 precondition per socaity-x8o — no first external ledger entry until V is final) · Related: socaity-bgl (declare the founder rate before the first grant budget) depends on this.

Issue:
- Context: sustainability.md "The backbone" records founder labor at a publicly declared rate, and M0–M2 contributions (manifesto edits, design docs, code, outreach) hit the ledger before any market or verification mechanism exists to price them. The founder both sets the rate schedule and is the largest epoch-share beneficiary — the exact "founder-tuned parameters that move founder income" conflict sustainability.md itself names.
- Question: (a) What is the valuation rule for heterogeneous pre-market contributions — a published rate card per contribution class, time-based at declared rates, or negotiated-per-entry with public reasoning? (b) What commitment device constrains the founder as price-setter — rate changes apply only prospectively, a cap on founder share per epoch, or third-party attestation of rates?
- Why it matters: Regressional Goodhart — credit for estimated value pays estimation error, and at M0 the estimator is the conflicted party. The manifesto's credibility with exactly the M0 cohort (people who read incentive designs critically) depends on this being visibly solved.

Adopted context binding this council (do not relitigate):
- socaity-x8o: conversion schedule V (versioned code) maps native units → valuation units, 1 vu = 1 standard contributor-hour; founder rate = V applied to founder hours, same table as everyone, citing a verifiable external referent; V fixed per epoch before it opens, amendable only for unopened epochs via the meta-rule; accrual is a pure function of a confirmed past observation. EpochOpened(1) — and therefore the first external entry — is mechanically blocked until V contains no placeholder rates.
- socaity-zjr: all entries provisional; pre-distribution audit; challenge process with enumerated grounds incl. misrepresented capacity.
- socaity-1ux: founder on the identical rule at a declared rate (uniformity covenant); parameters amendable prospectively only; epoch parameters immutable once the epoch opens.
- Remaining scope for THIS council: the shape of V's rate card for heterogeneous contribution classes, the observation units for non-timed work (is a design doc "hours"? attested how?), and the founder-conflict commitment device beyond what is already adopted.

## Round 1

### [mechanism-designer]
PROPOSAL (owner). **(a) V's rate card:**
**1. Flat cross-class rate: 1 attested hour = 1 vu for every class** (code, docs, outreach, design). No class multipliers at M0–M2 — not egalitarian sentiment but conflict surgery: any per-class differential is a founder-tunable parameter correlated with the founder's own work mix. Pre-market there is no non-conflicted estimator of skill differentials; the best commitment device is deleting the parameter. Differentials become priceable at M3+ prospectively via the meta-rule. External referent pins the vu→fiat meaning of "one standard contributor-hour" — one public source, pinned by version/date in V.
**2. Two observation-unit types, both settling in hours:** *Timed work* — attested via public worklog (timestamped log + corroborating trace: commits, drafts, call notes); hard cap 40 vu/person/week; all entries provisional under zjr. *Artifact work* (design doc, manifesto section, logo, launch post) — priced **prospectively via tickets**: each artifact enters the ledger only through a ticket opened before the work, in the versioned repo, carrying a vu budget from a published tier table (T1 minor ≤2 vu, T2 substantial ≤8 vu, T3 major ≤40 vu; exact bounds are V content, fixed per epoch). On acceptance, accrual = min(attested hours, ticket budget). Price precedes observation — bounty logic, not retrospective appraisal.
**3. No impact-based pricing of outreach** — impact pre-market is the most fakeable signal on offer; outreach settles in capped hours.
**(b) Founder-conflict device:** *Prospective-only changes* (adopted): necessary, insufficient. *Founder share cap:* **reject** — (i) breaks the uniformity covenant (a rule about who, not about the rate); (ii) at M0 the founder legitimately IS ~90% of labor — a binding cap forces the ledger to under-record truth, destroying the dogfooding thesis, while a non-binding cap is theater; (iii) creates a Sybil incentive for exactly the party controlling the identity layer (route hours through pseudonyms) — converts a transparency problem into a laundering problem. *Third-party attestation:* **adopt, strengthened** — EpochOpened(n) additionally requires a signed public attestation from ≥1 named non-beneficiary (no ledger position) that (i) V's rates match the cited referent within stated tolerance, (ii) no class multipliers, (iii) tier table unchanged from the draft that sat in public comment ≥14 days. Plus two uniform rules that bite the founder hardest because of size, without naming them: **concentration tripwire** (standing dashboard of per-contributor share; any contributor exceeding X% of an epoch's vu auto-escalates the zjr audit to full-worklog review) and **size-scaled challenge windows**.
**PARAMOUNT:** no retrospective negotiated valuation of finished work; no founder-settable class multipliers in any V covering epochs the founder contributes to; no hard founder share cap; no impact-priced units pre-market.
Partner questions: legal — wage-representation exposure of referent+attestation? community-builder — who attests at M0?

### [community-builder]
**1. The 30-second trust test dictates radical coarseness:** at most 5–7 classes in plain language, one anchor sentence ("1 vu = 1 standard contributor-hour, benchmarked to [referent]"), integer-ish multipliers if any. A finely-graded card *reads as* tuned-in-the-founder's-favor because at M0 only the founder could have tuned it. Simplicity IS the anti-Goodhart device — you cannot game a table a stranger audits in 30 seconds.
**2. First-contributor valuation: floor, don't appraise.** The typo fix is the M0 done-condition; its value is ceremonial and structural. Adopt a **minimum ledger entry (e.g. 0.5 vu) for any accepted contribution** — "the ledger records participation before it records magnitude." Substantial work: self-declared hours attested by the artifact, confirmable via the challenge process — never founder appraisal. The founder must never be seen appraising a stranger's work downward; zjr is where corrections happen, publicly. The first contributor's entry must not become a negotiation with the conflicted party.
**3. Founder-share optics: itemization + earliness story carries it; a cap backfires** — every parameter at M0 is founder-chosen, so a founder-chosen cap on founder share is theater the M0 cohort sees through ("who set the cap?"). What builds trust: founder hours logged at the same granularity, same evidence standard, same challengeability; a dashboard line "founder share: 100%, because N=1; here is the curve as others join." Plus: **founder entries challenge-eligible with a lower friction bar** (anyone can flag; founder must answer publicly).
**4. Device beyond adopted context:** the rate card is itself a node on the roadmap-graph with contestable edges; V amendments for unopened epochs sit in public as a proposal ≥14 days before adoption — converts founder-conflict from "trust me" into "watch me."
**PARAMOUNT:** no founder individual appraisal of external contributions before ledger entry; no rate card needing more than ~30 seconds/one screen; no founder-share cap presented as the trust device; no retroactive repricing of recorded entries ever.
CONSENT contingent on: minimum-entry floor, one-screen card, no founder pre-appraisal.

### [legal-counsel]
*(Analysis, not legal advice.)* **(a) Founder rate:** pre-incorporation only optics, but post-entity a director fixing self-flowing compensation is tegenstrijdig belang (WBTR — conflicted director may not participate in the decision); build the rule now as if the duty applied. Referent standard, descending strength: (1) **grant-funder published rates** (NLnet/NGI cost bases, STF day rates, Horizon personnel unit costs) — arm's-length, public, versioned, and what this project's actual funders pay for the same work; (2) CBS/market salary data acceptable if the role-mapping (occupation code, percentile) is itself published in V — the mapping is where discretion hides. Constraints: founder rate ≤ cited referent, cited with source/version/date in V's code; mapping challengeable under zjr; median-not-top is the cheap optics-buy.
**(b) Volunteer valuation traps:** deemed employment (7:610 BW, Deliveroo factors) — protective facts already adopted (no obligation, no direction, no enforceable claim at accrual, provisional entries); **hour-anchoring materially helps**: a vu is an internal measure of relative contribution to a contingent future distribution, not a euro owed — no wage, so minimum-wage floors have nothing to attach to. Keep substance AND vocabulary: V says "valuation of contribution," never pay/wage/compensation. Do NOT invoke the vrijwilligersregeling anywhere — market-benchmarked cards are incompatible with it; mixing framings is the worst position.
**(c) Tax:** income taxed when *genoten* (art. 3.146 Wet IB); provisional, contingent, non-transferable, audit-revocable entries against no obligor are very likely not yet enjoyed → **no present-day tax event from a high declared rate**; tax lands at distribution. Keep it that way: entries remain (i) contingent on inflows, (ii) non-transferable, (iii) defeasible until distribution. DGA wrinkle: a BV triggers gebruikelijk-loon deemed salary — one more argument for stichting-first. Material before first distribution: adviseur consult on the genietingsmoment.
**(d) Attestation standard:** a neutral third party must be able to **re-derive the vu figure from the artifact plus V alone**. Each non-timed entry: contemporaneous creation, immutable artifact link, V class invoked, public challenge window before confirmation. Founder entries: no self-attestation once any second attester exists; until then, publication + open challenge window IS the attestation — say so in V.
**PARAMOUNT:** no euro-denominated claims at accrual; no founder participation in deciding his own rate once an entity exists (referent + published mapping + challenge window is the substitute); no dual framing (rate card wins, drop volunteer rhetoric); no hardening of entries into unconditional claims pre-distribution.
CONSENT contingent on the four refusals reflected in V's draft.

### Facilitator
Standing proposal: MD's flat-rate V (1 attested hour = 1 vu, all classes; worklog-attested timed work capped 40 vu/wk; prospective ticket-priced artifact work with tier budgets; no impact pricing) + attestation gate on EpochOpened + concentration tripwire + size-scaled challenge windows + 14-day public comment on V amendments + CB's minimum-entry floor + CB's lower-friction founder challenges + legal's referent standard (grant-funder rates preferred, ≤ referent at median, mapping published and challengeable) + legal's vocabulary/tax/attestation constraints. Founder-share cap rejected by MD and CB independently; no role advocates it.
Deltas for Round 2: (i) CB's "5–7 classes with integer-ish multipliers" vs MD's flat single rate — MD's is strictly simpler; CB to confirm flat satisfies the one-screen paramount (classes remain as observation *categories* for legibility, all at 1 vu/hr). (ii) Ticket mechanics vs CB's no-founder-appraisal paramount: MD to confirm acceptance = merge/accept event with budget from the pre-published tier table and hours attested by the contributor — the founder gates *inclusion* (as any maintainer does) but never sets the *value*. Also: does the M0 typo-fix path need a pre-opened ticket, or does the floor (0.5 vu) apply ticket-free to trivial accepted contributions? (iii) CB to answer MD: who attests at M0 (a named non-beneficiary willing to sign — realistic recruitment path)? (iv) Legal to confirm the floor and tier tables don't undermine the hour-anchor (a 0.5 vu floor for a 5-minute fix is a valuation above attested hours — does min-entry conflict with re-derivability?).
Open objections: none standing; all consents conditional.
