# Council: socaity-7mk — Ledger contributor identity must be a user-held keypair from the first entry

Participants: identity-specialist, platform-engineer, legal-counsel
Type: decision · Priority: P0 · Blocks: socaity-5c5 (GDPR erasure), socaity-a8o (key loss/recovery), socaity-5d0 (fork-portable standing)

Issue:
- Context: milestones.md M0 ("ledger has recorded its first external contribution", "ledger always") and sustainability.md (founder labor on ledger from day zero). The ledger is append-only, public, and pays out retroactively at M5 — so whatever identifies a contributor in entry #1 is permanent.
- Question: What is the identity primitive for a ledger entry? Proposal on the table from the issue: contributors are identified by a self-generated keypair they hold (entries signed; platform stores only the public key plus an optional display name off-ledger). GitHub handles/emails as the primary key would foreclose pseudonymity, break fork portability, and put personal data on an unerasable ledger.
- Why it matters: This is the one identity decision M0 cannot defer. Retrofitting keys under recorded entries later means re-attributing value claims — exactly the dispute the ledger exists to prevent. Blocks the "done when" of M0.

Adopted context from socaity-zjr (relevant): ledger entries are provisional-by-default and adjudicated by a published process; entries and status changes are signed public entries.

## Round 1

### [identity-specialist]
PROPOSAL: **a self-generated Ed25519 keypair per contributor, held by the contributor, from ledger entry #1.**
1. **Key type:** Ed25519 (RFC 8032) — universal tooling, 32-byte keys, deterministic signatures, and can later serve as the binding key inside BBS+/Semaphore-style anonymous-credential issuance without re-keying (the M4 path). Contributor ID = multibase-encoded public key (did:key encoding, z6Mk…) — a W3C DID for free without running DID infrastructure. sig_alg is a versioned field (ed25519-v1).
2. **On-ledger (permanent):** public key, entry payload (contribution fact, node ref, capacity/artifact claim), timestamp, signature over canonical serialization (JCS), signed status-change entries per socaity-zjr. Nothing else — no names, emails, handles, avatars, free-text bios. A signed entry MAY include an opaque `attestation` field (hash of an off-ledger attestation object) — the M4 hook.
3. **Off-ledger (mutable, erasable):** profile keyed by public key: optional display name, avatar, linked accounts. GDPR erasure = delete the profile; contribution facts persist pseudonymously. Legal to confirm the Art. 17 balancing memo.
4. **Display names:** rendered from profile, non-unique, zero authority, key truncation always visible — name squatting worthless.
5. **GitHub linkage = attestation, not identity:** contributor signs `link:github:<user>:<pubkey>` with the ledger key, publishes it in a gist under that account; platform verifies both directions, stores the link off-ledger, revocable. Entries cite commit/PR URLs as evidence in the payload; attribution flows to the key. Pseudonymous contribution (artifact hashes as evidence) possible from day one.
6. **Founder included:** entry #1 signed by a founder-held keypair; no platform-god identity.
7. **Key loss/recovery (minimal M0):** optional pre-registered `successor` key; `rotate: old→new` signed by old or successor. Lost-key-no-successor: contributions stay with the orphaned key; re-attribution only via the socaity-zjr adjudication process as a signed public entry — recovery stays Sybil-auditable.
8. **Fork portability:** identity = user-held key + public ledger ⇒ forks inherit contributors with zero migration; platform holds no identity data a fork needs.
9. **M4 migration:** M0 keys are contributor identities (supply side), not personhood. At M4, personhood attestations are issued as anonymous credentials bound to the contributor key; demand-side actions require a personhood nullifier; supply-side entries never do. Forward-compat hooks (attestation field, versioned sig_alg) cost nothing now.
Platform stores in total: public ledger; erasable profile table; verified-link table. **No private keys, ever** — custodial keys silently become the default, and the default becomes the architecture.
**PARAMOUNT — not accepted:** (a) GitHub handle/email/platform account ID as primary key of a ledger entry, even "temporarily"; (b) any personal data written into the append-only ledger; (c) platform custody of private keys as a default flow; (d) personhood verification as a precondition for supply-side ledger entries at any milestone — personhood gates demand-side influence and M5 redemption, never the recording of work.
Trade-offs named: privacy ~zero cost; exclusion — keygen is real friction for non-developers, acceptable at M0 (contributors are developers), M1+ needs in-browser keygen; Sybil — deliberately unaddressed at M0 (entries unlock no conversion; adjudication is the filter; the budget equation activates at M4/M5).

### [platform-engineer]
PROPOSAL — I support the keypair primitive and hold a paramount line against alternatives. In socaity-zyt's schema, every entry carries actor_key; its semantics freeze at entry #1. M5 replays the log from the beginning; the forkability CI job (socaity-wg8) must verify attribution from public artifacts alone. If entry #1 says github:mlboogerd, attribution forever depends on GitHub's account database and the fork job can never verify without calling GitHub.
**NOT accepted (paramount):** (1) any platform-issued or third-party identifier as primary attribution key on-ledger; (2) platform-custodied private keys as the trust model — if the platform can sign as a contributor, "no dependence on privileged identity" is false and fork verification proves nothing; (3) any recovery mechanism that mutates or re-attributes past entries — recovery is forward-only; (4) **DID/blockchain machinery at M0** — W3C DID stacks, on-chain anchoring, wallets: substrate theater. Boring wins: Ed25519 + the existing hash chain + git-mirrored checkpoints.
Elements: actor_key = raw Ed25519 public key; every entry signed over the canonically-serialized entry (deterministic encoding is already mandatory for replay — signatures ride the same requirement); sig_algo field, additive-only. Off-ledger severable display-name mapping (matches the erasure constraint on socaity-zyt). **Rotation and loss are ledger events, not database edits:** key.rotated signed by old key; key.rebound issued through socaity-zjr's adjudication as a signed public entry linking new key to old standing — replay resolves identity continuity deterministically; no privileged mutation exists (this gives socaity-a8o its shape). Signing UX at M0 is near zero (two contributors; in-browser WebCrypto keygen + downloadable encrypted seed is a day of work); casual-custody is an M1+ UX layer (browser-held key, passphrase backup, optionally platform-stored *encrypted* blobs the platform cannot use) that never changes the schema — do not let custody anxiety push M0 toward handles-as-identity. Acceptance test: extend socaity-wg8 to verify every entry signature from exported artifacts with zero platform secrets. Flag: socaity-zyt v1 freeze is blocked on this council fixing actor_key semantics — specify canonical key encoding and the rotation/rebind event kinds.

### [legal-counsel]
Position (constraints + elements). **(1) GDPR status:** a public key identifying a contributor is *pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous* (Breyer; EDPB pseudonymisation guidance) — the keypair does not exempt the ledger from GDPR; it is nonetheless correct because it minimises the unerasable datum and keeps every strengthening of identifiability off-ledger and severable — the Art. 5(1)(c)/25 data-protection-by-design argument for the DPIA and any Art. 17 dispute (this council resolves the design half of socaity-m2i/5c5). Erasure = deleting all off-ledger linkage so the residual key is no longer reasonably attributable by the platform. Works only if the linkage genuinely is off-ledger and deletable.
**(2) Pseudonymity ends at the fiat rail, by law:** DAC7 due-diligence (name/address/TIN/DOB), annual tax statements (socaity-8u3), sanctions screening. Reconciliation is sequencing: the ledger records *earning* pseudonymously; identity binds *at claim time* by proving key control (signature challenge) to a KYC flow run by the licensed PSP partner; key→verified-identity records held off-ledger only as long as tax/AML retention law requires (Art. 17(3)(b) overrides erasure there — say so in the privacy notice from day one). A contributor may earn forever pseudonymously; they cannot be paid fiat pseudonymously. M0 comms must never promise otherwise.
**(3) NOT accepted (paramount):** handles/emails/legal names/**any user-supplied free-text field on-ledger** — including display names, entry memos, and dispute annotations (socaity-zjr status changes carry the same discipline); any key→identity linkage stored on-ledger or undeletable; any public statement that payouts will be pseudonymous or the ledger "GDPR-exempt"; platform custody of private keys as default.
**(4) Elements:** (a) schema rule enforced technically: on-ledger = public key, signature, structured contribution reference (hash/URI), amount, timestamp, status — no free text; (b) off-ledger profile is the sole platform-maintained linkage pre-M5, its deletion is the Art. 17 mechanism; (c) identity binding deferred to first fiat claim via PSP-run KYC against key-control proof; DAC7/tax/sanctions data lives with the PSP or a segregated retention-bound store, never on the ledger; (d) M0 contributor-facing terms state plainly: entries are permanent and public, erasure covers off-ledger data only, fiat payout requires identification; (e) risk-rank: no-PII-on-ledger schema rule is **blocking for M0**; DPIA material before M4; PSP/KYC flow material before M5.
Expect to CONSENT if the draft is the keypair primitive with these integrated.

### Facilitator
Standing proposal: identity-specialist's 9-point keypair primitive, which on its face satisfies both other roles' paramount constraints (no third-party ID as primary key; nothing personal on-ledger; no custody; forward-only recovery via ledger events; earning pseudonymous, payment KYC'd at claim time).
Deltas for Round 2: (i) **key encoding** — IS proposes did:key multibase encoding (as encoding only, no DID infrastructure); PE proposes raw Ed25519 bytes and holds a paramount line against "DID machinery" — is did:key-as-encoding inside or outside that line? Settle one canonical encoding for schema v1. (ii) **no-free-text discipline** — legal's schema rule (structured references only) vs IS's payload sketch ("contribution fact, capacity/artifact claim") and evidence URLs; confirm payload is structured fields + hashes/URIs, no free text. (iii) confirm event-kind names (successor/rotate vs key.rotated/key.rebound) so socaity-zyt can freeze v1. (iv) legal's M0 terms language (permanence, erasure scope, KYC-at-payout) — confirm it enters the M0 standing-commitment text alongside the socaity-zjr policy.
Open objections: none standing (all paramount positions currently conditional, none violated by the standing proposal).
