# Council: socaity-1ux — Epoch-share standing commitment wording: review by licensed NL counsel before M0 publishes it

Participants: legal-counsel, mechanism-designer, launch-strategist
Type: decision · Priority: P0 · Blocked by: socaity-h2c (mitigant constraints — subsumed here) · Related: blocks the M0 manifesto (socaity-im1)

Issue:
- Context: milestones.md M0 publishes the vision and starts the ledger; vision.md "Credit" and sustainability.md "The backbone" state a standing commitment that future inflows are distributed retroactively in proportion to recorded contribution, with epoch-share earliness premium.
- Question: What exact wording does the M0-published commitment use — "we commit to distribute" (entitlement-like) vs "we intend to make retroactive grants" (discretionary, RetroPGF framing)? Discretionary-until-declared wording plus non-transferability materially weakens participation-right/security characterization under NL law; entitlement wording published in the founding document is the single most quotable artifact in any future characterization dispute.
- Why it matters: BLOCKING for M0 — this text goes public at M0 and cannot be un-published; it underpins founder compensation and every early contributor's expectations. Mechanism-designer must confirm the discretionary framing does not destroy the earliness-premium incentive credibility; launch-strategist must keep all marketing copy consistent. A licensed NL practitioner reviews the final text — the one pre-M0 spend legal counsel insists on.

Adopted context binding this council:
- socaity-zjr resolution (Ledger Validation Policy): entries provisional-by-default; confirmation fixes eligibility and relative weight, never a money amount; amounts arise only upon declaration of a distribution; non-transferable; policy text publishes together with this commitment text inside the same counsel review.
- socaity-7mk resolution: pseudonymous keypair identity; M0 terms include permanence, erasure scope, KYC-at-payout.
- The council's product here is the commitment's substantive framing and the consistency rules for marketing copy — the final operative text still goes to licensed NL counsel (that engagement is the task remaining after this decision).

## Round 1

### [legal-counsel]
PROPOSAL. **The framing:** characterization risk splits along one line — discretion over *whether/when/how much* money flows vs discretion over *how it is allocated once declared*. Give away the first entirely; bind only the second: **no promise money will ever be distributed; public self-binding to the allocation rule if it ever is.** RetroPGF framing; also exactly what preserves incentive credibility. Ledger entries are records of fact, not instruments. NL anchors: avoids Wft deposit-taking (no repayable funds), profit-sharing-certificate analogy (no entitlement until declaration, earned not purchased, non-transferable), keeps compute credit out of EMD2/MiCA.
**Draft skeleton — "The Standing Commitment"** (plain-language clauses; operative text to licensed NL counsel): 1. What the ledger is (append-only public record, adopted identity primitive). 2. What recording means (provisional; confirmation fixes eligibility + relative weight only, never an amount). 3. What recording does not create (no debt/deposit/e-money/token/security/claim; non-transferable, non-purchasable; no monetary value unless and until declared; not an investment). 4. **The allocation rule — the binding part:** if a distribution is declared, the pool is allocated to confirmed entries in proportion to recorded contribution with the published epoch-share premium; rule + discounting algorithm published at M0, amendable prospectively only after open comment; confirmed weights never retroactively reduced except via validation/challenge. 5. **Declaration is discretionary** (whether/when/size at platform's sole discretion until declared; then payable per clause 4, challenged entries escrowed at declared weight). 6. Validation Policy incorporated as annex of equal rank — same document, same counsel review. 7. Identity terms incorporated per socaity-7mk (permanence, erasure scope, pseudonymous earning, KYC at claim, annual statements). 8. **No special founder position** (declared rate, same rules, same validation). 9. **Exit as enforcement** (ledger forkable; enforced by transparency and exit, not causes of action). 10. **Marketing consistency rule** — permitted lexicon: retroactive grants, recognition, allocation rule, recorded contribution, validation, challenge. Banned: dividend, return, yield, profit share, investment, equity(-like), guaranteed, "you will be paid", clawback. vision.md's "equity-like upside without issuing equity" must not appear in M0 operative text or marketing — the single most quotable sentence for an AFM characterization argument.
**PARAMOUNT:** no present-tense entitlement wording anywhere in M0 text; no transferability/purchasability now or by silent amendment; no publishing without the Validation Policy annex + identity terms in the same document; no operative text published before licensed counsel review; no interest/indexation/time-based accrual on undeclared amounts; no marketing copy outside the clause-10 lexicon.
Open questions: MD — does the clause-4/5 split preserve earliness-premium credibility, and is the epoch-share formula publishable at M0 in final prospective-only form? LS — is clause 10 enforceable across all launch surfaces including quoted founder speech?

### [mechanism-designer]
Discretionary framing does not inherently destroy the earliness premium — rational agents contribute against legally unenforceable expectations everywhere (RetroPGF, career equity). The incentive dies when discretion is *unbounded, opaque, or repriceable after the fact*, not when it is discretionary. Keep amount/timing discretionary; make allocation-conditional-on-distribution mechanically non-discretionary. **Minimum credibility mechanisms the M0 text must contain:**
1. **Rule-as-code, published at M0, versioned** — epoch boundaries, pie in claim units, share computation, premium formula as executable deterministic code; anyone recomputes every payout to the cent. Prose is where discretion hides; rule ambiguity is the #1 documented cause of retroactive-funding blowups.
2. **Epoch parameters fixed before the epoch opens** — immutable for that epoch; changes apply to future epochs only. The founder cannot dilute early epochs after seeing who is in them — closes the founder-tuned-parameter red flag by construction.
3. **Uniformity covenant** — founder compensation flows exclusively through the identical rule; the founder's stake is the hostage making discretion credible.
4. **Non-bypass commitment** — "we may never pay; we will never pay differently": if any inflow is distributed, only via the published rule. Kills the real fear (selective reallocation) rather than the tolerable one (delay).
5. **Forkability as enforcement, named in the text** — ledger + rule-code exportable at all times; abuse of discretion → community exits with the record; converts "trust us" into "trust us or leave with everything".
6. **Track-record device** — every inflow from M0 (grants explicitly) is ledgered and distributed or explicitly ledger-reserved under the rule; by M5 the commitment has a payment history. Say that grant routing *is* the rule operating.
**PARAMOUNT refusals:** discretion over relative allocation (repriceable denominator = claim worth zero to sophisticated contributors); prose-only rules; unversioned mutability or amendments to closed epochs; founder carve-outs; revocable ledger export.
Subject to legal confirming elements 2 and 4 do not tip into entitlement territory (they commit to *procedure*, not *payment* — I believe that is the safe zone, but the call is theirs): CONSENT to discretionary framing carrying these six elements.

### [launch-strategist]
**1. Two-register structure, both at M0:** plain-language register (manifesto, first screen) + operative register (counsel-reviewed text it links to). Plain must be a faithful compression — never softer, never bolder; quote-out-of-context test on the plain version alone (every sentence survives screenshot into an HN comment). Direction: *"Every contribution is recorded on a public, append-only ledger from day one. If money ever flows out of this project, it flows in proportion to what that ledger records — by a published rule, not by our mood. Whether and when distributions happen is not guaranteed; who they'd go to is fixed the moment you contribute."* Mechanism certain, money uncertain — the only framing satisfying both no-crypto-read and no-trust-me-bro, and it is true.
**2. Vocabulary standard (binding all copy, forever):** banned outright — token, coin, airdrop, tokenomics, mint, wallet, holders, "get in early", "upside", "equity-like" (never in public copy), APY/yield/staking, investment, returns; banned as entitlement triggers — guaranteed, "you will receive", owed, entitled, "your share of future revenue", dividend; required — ledger, record, contribution, retroactive, published rule, proportion, distribution (never "payout" in headlines), non-transferable, "you cannot buy this and you cannot sell it"; mandatory first-screen brand assets — "No token. Nothing to trade. The ledger is a database, not a blockchain."
**3. Credibility replaces promise-strength:** the answer to "trust me bro" is verifiable constraint, not stronger promissory language. Copy direction: *"You don't have to trust our intentions. The rule is public, the ledger is public, and if we ever betray it, fork it — everything you'd need comes with you."*
**4. Consistency rule:** the plain-language commitment is the *ceiling* for every future claim; one canonical phrasing reused verbatim; every credit/distribution asset checked against the banned list before publishing.
**PARAMOUNT refusals:** pure discretionary vagueness as first-screen text (rule-boundness of allocation carries the front screen; discretion language lives in the operative text); entitlement phrasing anywhere public; "equity-like upside"/"earliness premium" as headline copy or any percentage/multiple/projection of early contribution value (premium explained structurally: fixed pie per epoch, smaller network = larger share); headline overclaim with fine-print hedging (diverging registers = asset does not ship); legalese as first screen.
CONSENT contingent on the draft being compressible into the two-register structure.

## Round 2

### [legal-counsel]
AMENDMENT (consent once incorporated). **Element 2 (ex-ante immutable epoch parameters): safe zone, confirmed** — it constrains our rule-making procedure, not the money. Drafting discipline: state as a constraint on the rule ("parameters for an open epoch cannot be amended"), never as a right vesting in the contributor ("your share is locked in"). **Element 4 (non-bypass): safe in principle, entitlement-risky as drafted — two required amendments:** (a) scope to contributor distributions: "any discretionary distribution to contributors is made exclusively under the published rule; ordinary operating expenditure, contracted compensation, and grant-mandated spending are not distributions" — unscoped it reads as a quasi-trust over revenues, the exact profit-participation substance we avoid; MD's real target (selective reallocation) survives intact. (b) explicit no-third-party-rights clause: the commitment creates no stipulation for the benefit of third parties (art. 6:253 BW derdenbeding) and no cause of action; forkability and transparency are the sole enforcement — the clause keeping element 4 procedural rather than promissory. **Point (3): "who they'd go to is fixed the moment you contribute" fails on two counts** — factually wrong under the Validation Policy (weight fixes at *confirmation*, not contribution; an inaccurate plain sentence is both an entitlement trigger and a misrepresentation) and present-tense vesting language in the contributor's person. Required substitution: *"the rule that decides who they'd go to is already published — and once your contribution is confirmed, its recorded weight can't be rewritten."* Fixity attaches to rule and confirmed weight, not person or payment. CONSENT conditional on (a), (b), and the substitution; Round-1 paramount list carries unchanged including licensed counsel review before anything publishes.

### [mechanism-designer]
CONSENT, with one wording precision treated as clarification. Refusals check: (1) satisfied by clause 4 + non-bypass; (2) satisfied **provided the executable rule is authoritative over the prose in any divergence** — if prose controls, this reopens; (3) satisfied by ex-ante immutability, **but "prospectively" must be defined as "epochs not yet opened," not "not yet closed"** — amending an open epoch mid-flight is retroactive repricing in everything but name; one defined term fixes it; (4) satisfied by clause 8 + uniformity covenant; (5) satisfied provided export covers **ledger and rule-code together, at all times**. **Answer to legal's question (formula publishable at M0?):** Yes, with a distinction: the *structure* (fixed pie per epoch, share computation, premium curve's functional form) commits final at M0 as versioned code; *parameter values* (epoch length, decay rate) carry a designed amendment path because Goodhart discovery is a certainty. Safe construction: formula v1 is final and immutable for every epoch opened under it; amendments are version-tagged, apply only to not-yet-opened epochs, and follow an amendment procedure itself published at M0 as part of the rule. "The formula is final for any epoch it governs; the meta-rule for changing it is final, period" — stronger for characterization than frozen-forever, since future fixes are provably not exercises of allocation discretion.

### [launch-strategist]
**Clause-10 founder-speech answer: partially enforceable, honestly.** Fully enforceable on owned asynchronous surfaces (site, repo, posts, press, grant text) via pipeline. Not mechanically enforceable on live speech. Three-layer mechanism: (1) **pre-publication gate** — grep-able banned-wordlist in the repo, run in CI on site/manifesto content; canonical paragraph reused verbatim, never paraphrased; (2) **"the card"** — one-page briefing with the canonical paragraph plus prepared answers to the five predictable traps ("so it's like equity?", "what's it worth?", "when do I get paid?", "is this a token?", "how early is early enough?"); valuation questions always get the structural answer, never a number; (3) **correction protocol** — when the founder misspeaks live, publish a public correction linking the operative text; in a glass-house project a visible correction habit is a credibility asset and prevents any quote ossifying into the AFM's favorite exhibit. Question to legal: does a documented, consistent correction practice materially mitigate the quotability risk of stray founder speech? If yes, clause 10 is drafted as *pipeline-binding + briefing + correction duty*, not an impossible absolute over spoken words.
**AMENDMENT (point 3):** withdrawn sentence replaced. New canonical paragraph: *"Every contribution is recorded on a public, append-only ledger from day one. Records start as provisional and are confirmed through a published validation process. If money ever flows out of this project, it is allocated across confirmed records by a published rule, not by our mood. Whether, when, and how much is never guaranteed — no amount exists until a distribution is declared. What the rule protects is your recorded place in it: confirmed weights can't be quietly rewritten, and the whole ledger is forkable if we ever betray that."* Copy discipline addendum: "fixed"/"locked" may only ever modify *rule*, *epoch parameters*, or *confirmed weight* — never *share of money*, never *what you'll get*. CONSENT on the integrated proposal with the canonical paragraph (or legal's tightened redraft) as the single phrasing and clause 10 drafted per the three layers.

## Resolution

ADOPTED by consent, Round 3, no paramount objections standing.

**The M0 Standing Commitment instrument** — one document, licensed-NL-counsel-reviewed before publication, comprising the Standing Commitment (10 clauses), the Ledger Validation Policy annex (socaity-zjr), and the identity terms (socaity-7mk):

- **Framing:** "we make no promise that money will ever be distributed; we bind ourselves publicly to the allocation rule if it ever is." Retroactive-grant register throughout; ledger entries are records of fact, not instruments; no debt/deposit/e-money/token/security/claim; non-transferable, non-purchasable.
- **The binding half:** allocation rule + correlated-identity discounting published at M0 **as versioned executable code, authoritative over prose**; formula structure final for every epoch opened under it; parameter amendments version-tagged, applying only to **not-yet-opened epochs**, via a meta-rule itself published at M0. Confirmed weights never reduced except through validation/challenge.
- **The discretionary half:** whether/when/size of distributions at sole discretion until declared; upon declaration, payable per the rule; challenged entries escrowed at declared weight.
- **Non-bypass, scoped:** any discretionary distribution to contributors flows exclusively through the published rule; ordinary operating expenditure, contracted compensation, and grant-mandated spending are not distributions — with the precedence note that clause 8 (founder/insider compensation declared, arm's-length, same rules) controls wherever the carve-out could touch ledger participants.
- **No-third-party-rights clause:** no derdenbeding (art. 6:253 BW), no cause of action; transparency and forkability (ledger + rule-code exportable at all times) are the sole enforcement.
- **Track record:** every inflow from M0 (grants explicitly) is ledgered and distributed or explicitly ledger-reserved under the rule — stated as the rule operating, not goodwill.
- **Two-register publication:** operative text + plain-language canonical paragraph (adopted verbatim, LS Round-2 version with legal's operative-register variant), the ceiling for all future copy. Clause 10 vocabulary standard (merged banned/required lists; "equity-like upside" never in public copy; "fixed/locked" only ever modifies rule, epoch parameters, or confirmed weight) enforced as: CI banned-wordlist gate on owned surfaces + founder briefing card (five trap answers) + public correction protocol with a correction log as tripwire.

Follow-ups: engage licensed NL counsel on the full instrument (new task); socaity-h2c (mitigant lock) is satisfied by this resolution: earned-not-bought, non-transferable, discretionary-until-declared are now structural.
