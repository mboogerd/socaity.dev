The foundation · In development

Watane

Make what you need.
Leave a beginning for others.

Watane is being built as a home for small, personal tools called mods. Make one with a coding agent, use it alongside other people’s work, and share something the next person can build on.

That is where this ecosystem begins: with useful tools that can find each other. Applications for shared needs, beliefs, and plans can grow on that foundation.

Why start here? +

A tool can express an intention through what it makes possible. When someone adapts it, or connects it to another tool, cooperation happens through the work itself.

Watane’s design keeps the foundation small. Making needs and reasoning explicit belongs in the applications people build on it. Those applications are the next direction for exploration.
