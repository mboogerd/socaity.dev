## The headline: NGI Zero is over; NLnet's calls restart 2026-09-03

The single most important fact for this project's plan: **the NGI Zero
Commons Fund ran its thirteenth and final call, closing 2026-06-01, and no
longer accepts applications** `[V]`
(<https://nlnet.nl/commonsfund/>, accessed 2026-08-13). NGI Zero Core closed
its eighth and final call on 2024-10-01 `[V]` (<https://nlnet.nl/core/>,
accessed 2026-08-13). NGI TALER (14th call) and NGI Fediversity (12th call)
both closed their **final** calls on 2026-08-01 `[V]`
(<https://nlnet.nl/taler/>, <https://nlnet.nl/fediversity/>, accessed
2026-08-13).

As of today, **NLnet has no open call at all**: "There are currently no calls
open" `[V]` (<https://nlnet.nl/propose/>, accessed 2026-08-13). NLnet paused
submissions while it takes stock of a decade of NGI Zero and transitions to
the **Open Internet Stack** `[V]`
(<https://nlnet.nl/news/2026/20260612-NGIZero-stocktaking.html>, accessed
2026-08-13).

The restart, and the new deadline rhythm:

> "NLnet will resume its open calls on September 3rd 2026, with the first
> deadline on November 3rd." — `[V]`
> <https://nlnet.nl/news/2026/20260803-phaseshift.html>, accessed 2026-08-13

> "New calls for several funds will open up September 3rd 2026 with a
> deadline of November 3rd 2026 12:00 CEST (noon)." — `[V]`
> <https://nlnet.nl/propose/>, accessed 2026-08-13

The old "1st of every even month" cadence is dead. **Future deadlines are on
the third day of every odd month** (Jan/Mar/May/Jul/Sep/Nov) `[V]`
(<https://nlnet.nl/news/2026/20260803-phaseshift.html> and
<https://nlnet.nl/funding.html>, accessed 2026-08-13). The cached assumption
in the ticket ("next one is likely Oct 1") is wrong.

## NGI under the EU framework — 2026 status

- NGI-branded cascade funding is **winding down, not continuing**. Every NGI
  Zero / NGI TALER / NGI Fediversity call is now closed and each was
  explicitly labelled "final" `[V]` (NLnet fund pages, accessed 2026-08-13).
- <https://ngi.eu/opencalls/> still listed the NGI TALER 14th and NGI
  Fediversity 12th calls (both €5k–€50k, opened 2026-06-01, closed
  2026-08-01 12:00 CEST) as its most recent entries `[V]` (accessed
  2026-08-13). No post-August 2026 NGI call is listed.
- The successor is the **Open Internet Stack (OIS)**, an initiative of the
  European Commission's DG CONNECT, funded under **Horizon Europe** `[V]`
  (<https://ngi.eu/news/2025/08/04/from-ngi-to-the-open-internet-stack-charting-the-next-chapter-in-europes-digital-future/>,
  accessed 2026-08-13).
- NLnet was selected in January 2026 for three Horizon Europe grants —
  Restack (cascade funding), ELFA and CodeSupply (pilots) `[V]`
  (<https://nlnet.nl/news/2026/20260131-restack.html>, accessed 2026-08-13).
  Restack's own page cites Horizon Europe grant agreement No. 101299072 and
  €7M in grants to 2030 `[V]` (<https://nlnet.nl/restack/>); the January news
  item states a €10M budget for Restack `[V]` — the two figures differ (see
  Unverified).
- **Practical read:** the money and the people are the same; only the brand
  changed. Do not write "NGI Zero" in an application after 2026-09-03; write
  Restack / Open Internet Stack.

## Fit assessment for socaity.dev

**M1 (graph tool) → Restack, deadline 2026-11-03.** Restack is the only
programme in this calendar that (a) accepts an individual applicant, (b)
funds new development rather than maintenance of already-critical
infrastructure, (c) has a known near-term deadline, and (d) sits at €50k —
the right size for a first grant. The Sovereign Tech Fund's two explicit
exclusions ("we do not finance the development of prototypes", "not looking
for user-facing applications") rule socaity.dev out at its current stage;
revisit only once the graph tool is infrastructure other software depends on.

**M2 (observatory) → CodeSupply, or a second Restack proposal.** CodeSupply's
stated purpose — an open, federated catalogue of software metadata across
ecosystems — is the observatory's own ingestion problem, funded. This is a
better match than the generic Restack framing and is worth waiting one call
for if CodeSupply's first call lands after November.

**Prototype Fund is blocked on residency.** The German residency / GbR-seat
requirement survived the restructure unchanged. Unless the founder is a
German resident taxed in Germany (or the project incorporates a GbR seated
in Germany), Class 03 is not available. Its lead time (apply Nov 2026, money
June 2027) also makes it a poor bridge for M0–M1 even if eligible. Note also
that the headline €95,000 is the **team** ceiling; a solo applicant is capped
at **€47,500** — so even if the residency condition were solved, the realistic
figure for socaity.dev as a one-person project is roughly half of what a
€95k framing implies.

**Sovereign Tech Fellowship is a personal-income instrument, not project
funding**, and requires a pre-existing maintainer record on three FOSS
projects. Park it; re-check for a 2027 round around February 2027 (the 2026
round closed 2026-04-06).

## Recommended application sequence

Steps are listed by priority, **not** chronologically — step 5 (Prototype
Fund, opens 2026-10-01) falls between steps 2 and 3 on the calendar. In date
order the sequence is: 1 → 2 (09-03) → 5 (10-01) → 3 (11-03) → 4 (2027-01-03)
→ 6/7 (any time).

1. **Now → 2026-09-03.** Draft the Restack proposal for the M1 graph tool
   against the *preliminary* Guide for Applicants
   (<https://nlnet.nl/restack/guideforapplicants/>) and FAQ. Budget ≤€50k
   (first-time applicants are capped there). Emphasise: open-source licence,
   European dimension, and the Open Internet Stack framing.
2. **2026-09-03.** Calls open. Re-read the guide — a preliminary guide can
   change on opening day — then submit early rather than at the deadline.
   Also check on this date whether CodeSupply's first call opened alongside
   Restack.
3. **2026-11-03 12:00 CEST.** Hard deadline for the first cycle. If the M2
   observatory is far enough along, submit it as a **separate** proposal to
   CodeSupply (or Restack) in the same or next cycle — NLnet's lifetime caps
   (€500k Restack) leave ample room for two.
4. **2027-01-03 / 2027-03-03.** Next deadlines under the odd-month rhythm
   `[V pattern]` — the fallback if the November cycle misses, and the natural
   slot for the second (observatory) proposal.
5. **2026-10-01 → 2026-11-30.** Prototype Fund Class 03 — **only if** the
   German residency/GbR condition can be met. Do not build the plan on it.
6. **Opportunistic, any time.** Sovereign Tech Fund is rolling with no
   deadline; revisit once socaity.dev is depended-upon infrastructure rather
   than a prototype. ~6 months from submission to contract start `[V]`.
7. **Not in this calendar:** Optimism RetroPGF and Gitcoin rounds, named in
   sustainability.md, were out of scope for this pass and are unverified.

**Corrections applied by the evaluator:**

1. **Prototype Fund amounts (material).** The draft gave "max €95,000 over 6
   months for teams; Second Stage max €63,000". The page actually states:
   individuals max **€47,500**, teams max €95,000; Second Stage individuals
   max **€31,667**, teams max **€63,333**. The draft's €63,000 was wrong and
   it omitted the individual-applicant ceilings entirely — the load-bearing
   number for a solo founder. Table row and fit assessment both patched.
2. **Restack guide "preliminary".** The draft twice called the Guide for
   Applicants preliminary. It is not so labelled; the *fund page* is the
   provisional artefact. Unverified row corrected.
3. **Fellowship 2027.** The draft said no further round is announced
   anywhere; the page does signal recurring recruitment. Row corrected.
4. **Sequence ordering.** The recommended sequence is priority-ordered but
   reads as a timeline; an explicit date-ordered line was added.
5. **Prototype Fund fetchability.** The draft flagged the page as
   not-independently-cross-checked. The evaluator retrieved it successfully
   (browser user-agent defeats the 403); that caveat is now resolved.

**Not independently re-verified by the evaluator** (accepted as drafted, and

vs €10M discrepancy, the Sovereign Tech Challenge dormancy, ngi.eu open-calls

**Sourcing audit:** every claim in the body carries a URL and the 2026-08-13

**Sequencing consistency:** the recommendation (Restack for M1 by 2026-11-03,
