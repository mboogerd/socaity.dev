# Public-goods funding — domain knowledge

Verify current calls before relying on any deadline here — programs shift
yearly. This is the map, not the calendar.

## Funder profiles

- **NLnet / NGI Zero (Core, Commons Fund)**: €5k–€50k typical, rolling
  calls roughly every 2 months (deadline usually the 1st). Application is
  deliberately short (~2 pages of substance); reviewers are technologists
  who reward concreteness, open licenses, and internet-infrastructure
  relevance. Payment against completed milestones (memorandum of
  understanding with task list — the fundable-unit decomposition *is* the
  application). Free additional services (security audit, accessibility
  review, packaging) — take them, they're credibility assets. NL-based
  founder + open-source + commons framing = strong natural fit. Best
  first target for M1 (graph tool) and M2 (observatory) as separate
  applications.
- **Sovereign Tech Fund (now Sovereign Tech Agency)**: Germany; funds
  *maintenance and improvement of existing critical open infrastructure*,
  €50k–€500k. socaity itself is too new — but this is the observatory's
  *customer*: STF's "General Fund" selection problem ("what is critical
  and under-maintained?") is literally what the M2 index computes. Pitch
  as data partner / methodology collaboration, not as grantee, until
  there's maintained infrastructure to point at. Also: the index should
  make STF's portfolio choices citable evidence ("STF funded 7 of our
  top 20").
- **Prototype Fund (DE)**: individuals/small teams, ~€47.5k / 6 months,
  requires German residency — likely excluded for an NL founder; check
  the (post-2024 restructured) program's current rules before writing it
  off entirely.
- **EU NGI successors / Horizon cascades**: NGI's shape changed after
  2024; watch for cascade-funding calls (open calls from Horizon projects
  distributing small grants). More paperwork per euro than NLnet; second
  priority.
- **Optimism RetroPGF / Gitcoin rounds**: crypto-ecosystem money.
  Philosophically aligned (the vision cites both) but brand-sensitive —
  taking crypto-ecosystem funds while holding a hard no-token line needs
  a prepared public rationale (with `launch-strategist`). Retro rounds
  reward *shipped* impact: relevant from M2, not before.
- **Foundations (Ford/Sloan/Omidyar digital infrastructure, Mozilla,
  OTF)**: larger, slower, relationship-driven; the M2 index + press is
  the door-opener. Cultivate from M2, expect M3+ money.

## Application checklist (NLnet-shaped, reusable)

- [ ] One-paragraph problem statement a non-expert funder reads cold and
      gets. Lead with xz/log4j concreteness, not mechanism abstraction.
- [ ] Work packages: 5–8 deliverables, each independently verifiable,
      each with a budget line and a month estimate. Grants pay for M1/M2
      artifacts, not for "the vision".
- [ ] Ask covers founder labor at the declared ledger rate (dogfooding:
      the public rate *is* the budget justification).
- [ ] Everything open-licensed (AGPL/Apache/CC — have the answer ready;
      NLnet requires open outcomes).
- [ ] Comparison-with-existing-efforts section: name Libraries.io,
      criticality score, CHAOSS honestly (reviewers know them; omission
      reads as ignorance).
- [ ] Sustainability answer: sustainability.md is unusually strong
      material — funders rarely see a costed post-grant plan; use it.
- [ ] Named risks with mitigations (reviewers trust applicants who name
      their own risks).

## Pipeline discipline

- Sequence: NLnet application for M1 tool now; separate NLnet application
  for M2 observatory next call (two focused asks beat one sprawling one);
  STF data-partnership conversation once index prototype exists; retro
  rounds after M2 ships.
- Track per application: call, deadline, ask, work packages, status,
  reviewer correspondence. Keep as beads issues (`role:grant-writer`,
  `task`) so the pipeline is on the public ledger like everything else.
- Rejections: request feedback, revise, resubmit next call — NLnet
  explicitly welcomes resubmission and it works.

## Reporting

- Grants enter the ledger attached to the nodes they fund
  (sustainability.md rule); milestone completion evidence = links to
  shipped artifacts + public ledger entries. Design reports to be
  assembled from the glass house in an afternoon, not written fresh.
