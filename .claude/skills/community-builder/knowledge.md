# Community building — domain knowledge

## Cohort playbooks per milestone

- **M0 manifesto believers**: the conversion event is *reading*, so the
  ask must be immediate and small: star/watch the repo, join one channel,
  claim a ledger entry by contributing anything (a typo fix counts — the
  point is the first external ledger entry, per M0's done-condition).
  Channel choice: one place, not five. A single Zulip/Discourse beats
  Discord+Slack+Matrix fragmentation; archives must be public and
  indexable (glass house).
- **M1 tool users**: developers adopt tools, not movements — the movement
  is discovered later ("come for the tool, stay for the network"). Their
  first-run experience is `product-designer`'s job; their *second week*
  (support responsiveness, issue turnaround) is yours. Early user issues
  answered in hours build the reputation that M3 consent asks draw on.
- **M2 OSS-sustainability world**: this cohort is reached through
  relationships, not broadcast: SustainOSS forum/calls, FOSDEM community
  devroom, Maintainer Week events, CHAOSS community, foundation staff
  (Sovereign Tech, OpenSSF, Ford/Sloan digital-infrastructure circles).
  Show up contributing before asking for anything. The index makes this
  cohort's argument *for* them — position it as their evidence, not your
  product.
- **M3 agent-owners**: found where agentic tooling is discussed (Claude
  Code/Cursor/aider communities). Their motivation mix: meaning, credit,
  demonstration of their setup's capability. Leaderboards work here —
  ledger standings are a natural, transparency-compatible leaderboard.
- **M4 need-havers**: the first non-developer cohort; recruit through
  concrete needs communities, not "the public". Pick 2–3 seed verticals
  where unmet software needs are articulate (e.g. accessibility tooling,
  small-nonprofit ops, local government transparency).

## Maintainer relations (the trust-critical path)

- Before the index publishes: personal email to every listed maintainer,
  2+ weeks ahead, with their dossier, the correction path, and the offer
  to be removed. Being named "under-supported" must land as advocacy with
  an exit, never exposure. Expect and welcome "actually we're fine, Google
  pays two of us" corrections — each one improves the index
  (`data-analyst` needs these).
- M3 consent ladder: (1) maintainer opts the project in, (2) picks/blesses
  target issues, (3) sets etiquette (issue-first, disclosure format),
  (4) retains a visible kill switch. Start with 3–5 projects where a real
  relationship exists; depth over count — the M3 done-condition needs one
  genuine story, not fifty shallow ones.
- Never argue with a maintainer in public, even when wrong. The OSS world
  is small and elephant-memoried.

## Retention between milestones

- Build-in-public cadence (with `launch-strategist`): weekly short updates
  in one canonical place, mirrored out. The ledger itself is content —
  "this week's contributions" is auto-generatable and genuinely novel.
- Give believers structural work before the tool exists: the roadmap *is*
  the first needs graph (M0), so early argument about decomposition and
  edges is both engagement and dogfooding. Contested edges by design.

## Governance culture (pre-write, don't improvise)

- Ship at M0: code of conduct (Contributor Covenant is fine), decision
  records in public (the docs already model this), a dispute path for
  contested edges (structured claim → counter-claim → cool-off →
  resolution recorded; escalation to founder as the visible-but-rare
  backstop).
- Ostrom mapping (shared with `mechanism-designer`): graduated sanctions =
  rate limits before bans; monitoring = public logs; cheap exit = fork
  docs that are genuinely maintained. Moderation actions on the public
  record — a glass house cannot have a back room.

## Metrics that matter (and the vanity ones to ignore)

Track: time-to-first-response on issues; second-contribution rate (the
single best community-health predictor); cohort-to-cohort conversion
(manifesto reader → tool user, etc.); maintainer NPS after M3 contact.
Ignore: stars, Discord member counts, impressions.
